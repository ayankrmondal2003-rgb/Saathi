Your account

  • Give accurate information, and keep your sign-in and Safety PIN private.
  • Only add people to your circle who know you and agree to receive your alerts.
  • You're responsible for the messages you send from your own phone, including SOS texts to your contacts.

Using SOS responsibly

  • Start an SOS only when you need help. To test Maitri, tell your circle first and end the SOS with your PIN.
  • Don't start false alarms, harass anyone, or use Maitri to track someone without their knowledge.
  • To prevent abuse, each account can start at most 3 SOS alerts an hour.

  • Only accept a request if you can respond safely. You are never required to put yourself at risk; call 112 when someone is in danger.
  • Use a person's location and details only to help them during that request.
