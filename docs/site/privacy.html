MAITRI

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Maitri Android app (beta) and its server.

Maitri is a beta and is not an emergency service. It does not contact the police or call 112 for you. If you are in danger, call 112.

Maitri helps you reach your trusted circle and nearby verified volunteers when you need help. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to get it or delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data for anything other than running Maitri.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Account: your name and email (or the details from Google sign-in), and a Firebase account idTo sign you in and keep your circle linked to you.
Profile you choose to add: phone number, city, profile photoSo your circle can recognise and reach you. Your phone number is shown to a responder only if you turn on "Let responders who accept my SOS call this number".
Medical notes (optional)Shown to a responder who has accepted your SOS, only while it is active. Stored encrypted.
Trusted contacts: names and phone numbers you enter or pickTo alert them when you start an SOS.
Location: precise location while an SOS is active and while you share a trip. Volunteers who turn on "Available": their location when they open Maitri and, only if they chose "Allow all the time", about every 15 minutes while the app is closedTo show where you are to the people helping you, and to find available volunteers near an SOS. A volunteer's location is used only to decide whom to ask for help; nobody sees it unless they accept a request. Turning "Available" off stops it. Maitri does not track you in the background at any other time.
SOS voice notes and photos you choose to recordSo the people in your circle who were alerted about that SOS can hear and see what is happening, and so you keep a record of it. Volunteer responders never see them.
Volunteer documents (ID, address proof, police certificate, photo, declaration)So a Maitri reviewer can verify volunteers before they receive SOS requests.
Device notification tokenTo deliver SOS requests and circle alerts to your phone.
Notifications, check-ins and trips you createTo show them in the app and alert your circle if you miss a check-in.
Crash reports and error logs (no message content)To find and fix problems.

Who can see what

  • Your circle (people you linked with an invite code): your SOS alert with a live-location link, trips you share with them, and missed check-ins.
  • Contacts without the app: only what you send them yourself from your phone's messaging app. The live-location link works only while your SOS is active and shows no name or contact details.
  • Nearby volunteers: before accepting, only an approximate distance (for example "Under 500 m"). After accepting, until the SOS ends: your location, first name, medical notes, and your phone number if you allowed it.
  • You, when you help someone: the same, in the other direction. The person you help sees your first name, status and approximate distance.
  • Maitri reviewers: volunteer applications and documents, through links that expire after 5 minutes. Every view is logged.

How long we keep it

  • SOS location: erased 7 days after the SOS ends.
  • SOS voice notes and photos: deleted 30 days after they were recorded.
  • Notifications and SOS audit records: 30 days.
  • Trips and check-ins: 7 days after they end.
  • Everything else (account, profile, contacts, volunteer application): until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile & settings › Privacy & data › Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile & settings › Privacy & data › Delete my account, or see Delete your account. This deletes your data from our database and storage and your sign-in.
  • Correct your details: Profile & settings › Edit profile.
  • Withdraw permissions (location, notifications, microphone) in your phone's settings at any time.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For questions, complaints or to exercise your rights, contact our grievance contact at ayankr.mondal6296@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Medical notes are encrypted in our database. Files are kept in private storage and opened only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at the address above if you find a problem.

Children

Maitri is not meant for children under 13. Volunteers must be 18 or older.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
