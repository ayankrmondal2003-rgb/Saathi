Delete your Maitri account

You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Maitri and go to Profile › Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Delete my account and sign in again when asked.
  3. If an SOS is open, end it with your Safety PIN first.

Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email ayankr.mondal6296@gmail.com from the email address you use with Maitri, with the subject "Delete my Maitri account". We'll confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in, profile, photo and medical notes
  • Your trusted contacts and circle links (you leave other people's circles too)
  • Your SOS history, locations, voice notes and photos
  • Your volunteer application and documents
  • Your notifications, trips, check-ins and device registration

Nothing is kept afterwards. Copies in encrypted backups made by our database provider expire on their normal schedule (at most 7 days) and are never restored for individual accounts.
