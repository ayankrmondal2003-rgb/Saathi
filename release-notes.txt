Maitri 0.9.2-beta.3 - design fixes and mutual circles

What's new:
- Circle: "Circles you're in" shows whose SOS alerts reach you. Tap "Add ... to my circle too" to send them a request; once they accept, you're in each other's circles.
- Circle: "Import from phone contacts" now picks a phone number correctly.
- Responder: a live map of where you are, with the 1 km area requests come from while you're Available.
- Responder: your volunteer status at the top (Verified volunteer, under review, or not approved with the reviewer's note).
- Dark mode: all text is readable; switches and buttons use Maitri's pink instead of purple.
- New Themes dialog (Profile > Themes), a branded loading animation, and a cleaner bottom bar.

Reminders:
- SOS here is REAL: your circle gets an alert and your live location. Don't hold the SOS button for fun.
- In a real emergency, call 112.
- Report problems from Profile > Help & support, or reply to the invite email.
