Maitri 0.9.1-beta.2 - fixes from two-phone testing

What's new:
- An SOS never gets stuck on "Connecting…": if Maitri was closed during an SOS (update, restart, battery), it now reconnects within seconds. If it still can't reach Maitri, it tells you to call 112.
- Volunteers: requests can now wake your screen. Allow it from the Responder tab ("Let SOS requests wake your screen").
- Volunteers: requests clear themselves when the SOS ends or expires.
- Volunteers can be reached while Maitri is closed (optional background location, every 15 minutes).

Reminders:
- SOS here is REAL: your circle gets an alert and your live location. Don't hold the SOS button for fun.
- In a real emergency, call 112.
- Report problems from Profile > Help & support, or reply to the invite email.
