Maitri 0.9.3-beta.4 - faster start, clearer screens, SOS requests you can't miss

What's new:
- A new "Getting things ready" screen while Maitri prepares everything at once. Screens now open instantly with your last data, even offline.
- If you have an SOS open, Maitri goes straight to it when you open the app.
- Light mode: cards are easier to see.
- Responder tab: everything in clear cards, with a "Request alerts" check that shows what to fix so SOS requests reach you.
- Volunteers: an SOS request now pops up over any app, and over the lock screen if you allow it, with Accept and Decline right there. It keeps vibrating until you answer (turn this off in Profile > Keep vibrating for SOS requests). If the SOS ends, it tells you.
- Smoother, faster animations.

Reminders:
- SOS here is REAL: your circle gets an alert and your live location. Don't hold the SOS button for fun.
- In a real emergency, call 112.
- Report problems from Profile > Help & support, or reply to the invite email.
