Maitri 0.9.4-beta.5 - small polish

What's new:
- Opening your profile or your notifications now shows the page softly blurred with the Maitri logo beating while it loads.
- Volunteers: if an update turns off "SOS requests on the lock screen", Maitri reminds you with a notification. Tap it to turn it back on, so a nearby SOS request can still wake your screen.

Reminders:
- SOS here is REAL: your circle gets an alert and your live location. Don't hold the SOS button for fun.
- In a real emergency, call 112.
- Report problems from Profile > Help & support, or reply to the invite email.
